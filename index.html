About

I am a researcher working on sequential decision-making and simulation optimization. My research focuses on developing efficient algorithms for identifying high-quality decisions under uncertainty, especially when performance and risk constraints are both important.

More broadly, I am interested in the intersection of operations research, machine learning, stochastic simulation, and emerging computational methods.

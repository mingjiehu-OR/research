Privacy & optional analytics

This academic website is maintained by Mingjie Hu. You can read every page and follow every paper link without enabling analytics.

Your choice

Google Analytics 4 is loaded on the homepage only after you select Allow. Selecting Decline leaves analytics off. Before consent, this site's analytics code does not contact Google or set analytics cookies. This privacy page does not load analytics.

Your choice is remembered in this browser's local storage for up to 180 days. If browser storage is unavailable, it applies only to the current page. You can change your analytics choice at any time. Declining after previously allowing stops further collection and removes this site's analytics cookies. The homepage then reloads unless the browser cannot save or clear the old choice; in that case analytics stays off and a warning is shown. Withdrawal does not erase data already processed by Google.

What is measured

With your permission, Google processes page visits, outbound-link clicks and clicks on supported file links, along with browser/device information, approximate location and referral source. These help me understand how people find the site and which papers they visit. A paper-link click does not necessarily mean that a paper was downloaded or read.

Analytics cookies use the mh_research prefix, are scoped to this site's /research/ path and last up to 90 days. They help distinguish browser visits, not identify you by name. The homepage URL is sent without query parameters or its section fragment, and referrers are limited to their website origin. No names, email addresses or other identifiers are deliberately added to analytics events.

Google signals and advertising personalization are disabled. I do not use these statistics for advertising, and no visitor counter is displayed publicly. Read how Google uses information from sites that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Hosting and external links

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process technical request information independently of optional analytics. See GitHub's Privacy Statement. Paper repositories, Google Scholar and other external websites have their own privacy practices when you follow their links.
